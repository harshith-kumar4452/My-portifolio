02Web · AI

MAARG — AI recovery for misplaced shipments

Predicts where parcels go missing, prices each delay in rupees per hour, and runs a reverse auction so vehicles already heading that way carry the recovery. A FastAPI backend runs nine engines, including a misplacement classifier, a time-expanded router and a counterfactual explainer, on PostgreSQL. Four role-based portals sit on top.
